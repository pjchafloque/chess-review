# Background music

- `gymnopedie-1.mp3`: Erik Satie, Gymnopédie No. 1 (1888), 3:21.
- `gymnopedie-3.mp3`: Erik Satie, Gymnopédie No. 3 (1888), 2:49.

The compositions are in the public domain. The notes come from the Mutopia Project's editions
(Gymnopédie No. 1: Mutopia-2014/12/14-37; No. 3: Mutopia-2012/12/23-39), typeset by Evin Robertson
and placed in the public domain by the typesetter (https://www.mutopiaproject.org).

Piano: Salamander Grand Piano V2 (a Yamaha C5) by Alexander Holm, licensed CC BY 3.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Samples from https://github.com/Tonejs/audio.

Rendered by `scripts/music/lyparse.py` (reads the LilyPond files) and `scripts/music/render.py`
(plays them on the samples with pedalling, a little rubato, a synthetic hall reverb), then encoded
to MP3 with ffmpeg. The renders are released under CC BY 3.0 as well, crediting the above.
